How PLMunTrack Works

A guided flow for registration, authorization, and attendance recording.

STEP 01

Register

Complete the appropriate student or visitor registration form.

STEP 02

Generate QR

Generate the QR code using the configured system workflow.

STEP 03

Verify Authorization

Check visitor authorization and visit-date rules before accepting entry.

STEP 04

Record Activity

Use the scanner to process supported QR tokens and record attendance events.

System information

PLMunTrack depends on its configured Google Apps Script, Google Sheets database, token validity, and authorization rules. Verify that each scan is recorded correctly in the database.
