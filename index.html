How PLMunTrack works

A guided flow for supported registration, authorization, and attendance tasks.

STEP 01

Register

Use the appropriate registration workflow to provide the required information.

STEP 02

Generate QR

Generate a QR token through the configured system workflow.

STEP 03

Authorize

Visitor access must be authorized before the visitor scan is accepted.

STEP 04

Record

Use the scanner to process valid tokens and record supported attendance events.

Access and data security

Actual results depend on the configured Google Apps Script, Google Sheets data, token validity, and authorization rules. Do not share administrator secrets or sensitive personal information.
