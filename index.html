How It Works

A Simple Attendance Process

The system organizes the basic steps for registering users and recording attendance.

1

Register

Add the required student information through the registration page.

2

Generate QR

Generate or retrieve the QR token associated with the registered student.

3

Scan QR

Scan a valid token to submit an attendance record.

4

Review Records

Open the dashboard to review the available attendance information.

🔒

Responsible Use of Information

Use the system only for authorized attendance and visitor monitoring activities. Ensure that personal information is handled responsibly and that access is limited to authorized users.
